TestLoop Terms of Use

Last updated: October 2026

1. What TestLoop is

TestLoop is an online platform operated by Alexander Anthony Joseph ("we", "us"). Android developers pay a fee to list an app for closed testing. Other people ("testers") join a test, use the app on separate days for 14 days, and give honest written feedback. Testers earn a payout for each completed test.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. You must give true information and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens in your account. One person may keep one account.

3. Money

  • Added by card: money you add to your wallet by card can be used to list games. Unused card-added money can be refunded on request within 30 days of adding it, minus the payment provider's fees.
  • Listing fee: the fee to list a game is shown in the app before you pay. Once testers have joined your game, the fee is not refundable.
  • Tester payouts: a tester earns the amount shown on the game, after finishing all 14 days and sending written feedback. Earned money can be withdrawn once it reaches the minimum shown in the app.
  • Withdrawals are checked by us and paid manually, usually within 7 working days. You choose how to be paid and you give correct payout details. We are not responsible for money sent to details you entered wrongly.
  • Card payments are handled by Flutterwave. We never see or store your card number.
  • Prices can change. A change does not affect games already listed.

4. Honest testing

Testers must really install and use the app they test. Fake, automated, copied or repeated check-ins and feedback are not allowed. Developers must not pay or pressure testers for good reviews, and must not use TestLoop to break Google Play's rules.

If we think an account is cheating, we can remove it, cancel its tests, and hold or cancel its unpaid earnings.

5. No guarantee of Google approval

TestLoop gives developers real testers and a report of the tests. It does not guarantee that Google will approve an app or a production access request. Google makes that decision, and its rules can change.

6. Content

Feedback you write must be your own, honest and lawful. Developers may see testers' feedback in their test report, but never the tester's name or email. We may remove content that breaks these terms.

7. Our responsibility

We run TestLoop with reasonable care, but we provide it "as is". We do not promise it will always be available or error-free. To the extent the law allows, our responsibility to you is limited to the money you paid or earned through TestLoop in the 12 months before the problem.

8. Ending your account

You can stop using TestLoop at any time and ask us to close your account. We can close accounts that break these terms. Earned money that is not held for cheating will be paid out when you close your account.
