TestLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

TestLoop is operated by Alexander Anthony Joseph. Contact: alexander85j@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and an encrypted password.
  • Wallet and activity: money added, fees paid, amounts earned, games you list, tests you join, your check-in days and the feedback you write.
  • Payout details: when you request a withdrawal, the payout method, account details, name and country you enter.
  • Payments: Flutterwave processes card payments. We receive only confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount and a reference. We never receive your full card number.
  • Technical data: your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Our hosts may log basic technical data such as IP address and time of request.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account, wallet, tests and payouts.
  • To check payments and prevent fraud and fake testing.
  • To show developers a test report.
  • To contact you about your account or a payout.

4. Who sees it

  • Developers see the number of testers, check-in days and written feedback for their own games. They do not see testers' names or emails.
  • Testers see a game's name and its opt-in link after joining.
  • Service providers that run TestLoop: Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in London), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Flutterwave (payments).
  • We do not sell your data. We share it with authorities only if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. After you close it, we delete or anonymise it, except records we must keep for payments, tax or fraud checks.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, or delete your account and data. Email us from the address on your account and we will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are stored encrypted. Money changes and payment checks happen on our server, not in your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

TestLoop is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
